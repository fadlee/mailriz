What self-hosting actually costs

We spoke to three people who moved off managed services last year. None of them did it to save money, and two of them did anyway — but the savings were not where they expected.

"The bill went from ninety a month to about four. The part nobody warns you about is that you are now the one who gets paged."

The pattern that held across all three: the cost moves from money to attention. Whether that is a good trade depends entirely on how much of the latter you have.
